## Data Wrangling
Preparing data for analysis. Wrangling may include, but is not limited to, ensuring data is specified as the correct data type, evaluating and handling missing values, and checking for inconsistencies in the data. Data wrangling may also be refered to as data cleaning.

## Date Shifting
A process of shifting dates by a certain, usually random, amount to ensure data privacy.

## Domain Expert
People who have specialized knowledge in a field (i.e. domain) of interest. For example, domain experts might include medical doctors, physicists, CEOS, lawyers, etc.

## Impute
The process of filling in missing values. Many techniques may be used to impute, but a simple method might include replacing a missing value with the overall mean or median value for a given variable.

## Forward Filling
An imputation technique referring to when the last observation is carried forward to replace a missing value. 

## Backward Filling
An imputation technique referring to when the next observation is carried backward to replace a missing value.
